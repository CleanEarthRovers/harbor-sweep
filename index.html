West Harbor · San Pedro

Harbor Sweep

Trash floats into the harbor every day. Pilot the robot that scoops it up before it reaches the ocean. First you steer. Then the rover drives itself.

About 90 seconds. Student concept by a USC WRIT 340 ATLAS team for Clean Earth Rovers.
